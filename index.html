I learned what “a process” really means in retail: invoices that don’t match the delivery, refunds, daily sales reports, a support inbox full of the same questions. Now I automate exactly those things with AI.

For the last half year I have worked on independent AI projects: prompts for ChatGPT, Claude and Chinese models such as DeepSeek and Qwen, AI assistants for business workflows and automations in n8n and Make. Before that I worked as an SQL analyst and manual QA, and for two years as a shift manager whose store reached the global top 15 of its chain.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Applied Economics from Comenius University in Bratislava. Java is my main language, with SQL, C++ and Rust next to it. Every project below is on GitHub with its source code.
